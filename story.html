A look back

The Funkyfreshe story

One pair of earrings and a couple of curious kids at the pool. Here's how a poolside idea grew into a table full of handmade things, one market at a time.

July 2021

The idea

The kids Samantha lifeguarded over the summer kept noticing her "funky" and "fresh" earrings. The nickname stuck, and so did the idea.

August 2021

Etsy opens

Funkyfreshe went online. The first handmade pairs shipped out to strangers across the country.

July 2022

Sammie's first in-person booth

A folding tent, a table of earrings, and a whole lot of nerves. The first in-person booth at Cincinnati's beloved City Flea.

July 2023

Second summer of the City Flea

Back for round two, now a regular fixture in front of Cincinnati Music Hall. The booth got bigger and the regulars started showing up.

July 2024

Third summer + printed sheets

A third summer at the Flea, and a new trick: printed shrink-film designs (hello, Eras Tour) that let the collection get a little louder.

March 2025

Closing the Etsy shop

College moved Samantha to Cornell, and keeping up with online orders from a dorm room wasn't realistic. The Etsy shop closed, but the brand didn't slow down.

😢
July 2025

Back at the Flea + watch purses

Still vending all summer at the City Flea, and debuting something new: watch purses, straps built from vintage watch faces.

December 2025

Cornell Creators Market

Funkyfreshe went to college too, joining the Cornell Creators Market for the very first time.

March 2026

First banner sale

The first proper banner sale.

April 2026

Cornell Creators Market, again

Back at the Creators Market with a sharper booth and a full table of new designs.

August 2026

A home of its own

Five years in, Funkyfreshe gets a website. People kept asking where to find the purses, so now there's one hub to see what's available and buy directly. You're looking at it.

Meet the maker

Samantha Neeb

Founder & maker, rising Cornell senior studying Animal Science with a double minor in Business and Communication. Samantha is the creator and curator behind Funkyfreshe, with equal parts science brain and creative energy, bringing both to every pair — and every purse.
